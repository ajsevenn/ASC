Delta Air Lines

Schedule Change Rebooking Threshold

Domestic03H:00M or 180 minutes
International06H:00M or 360 minutes

Minor Schedule Change

If the applicable change is less than 180 minutes for a domestic itinerary or less than 360 minutes for an international itinerary, it does not meet the significant time-change threshold. If no other qualifying condition applies, accept the new flight, document the notification or contact attempt, and send the updated itinerary through the airline so the Pax can view the new flight details. If the call is routed to voicemail, document the contact attempt and send the updated itinerary.

Major or Significant Schedule Change

If the applicable schedule change is 180 minutes or more for a domestic itinerary or 360 minutes or more for an international itinerary, the Pax may be eligible for complimentary rebooking or a refund under Delta's policy.

Option 1: Pax Wants to Rebook

Reissue code or instruction: OSI DL REISSUE DUE TO DL INVOL

Placement: Add the OSI message to the PNR, then reissue according to Delta's policy. If Delta already revalidated the ticket and the Pax accepts the change, add OSI DL ACCEPT DUE TO DL INVOL.

Applies to: Eligible itineraries on Delta 006 ticket stock covered by the current policy.

Reissuing Conditions

  • Reconcile the PNR by removing the old or changed segments.
  • If the PNR and electronic ticket match because Delta already revalidated the ticket, do not reissue. Add OSI DL ACCEPT DUE TO DL INVOL after the Pax accepts.
  • If reissue is required, keep the same origin and destination, or use an allowed alternate airport within 100 miles.
  • Book the same cabin. Use the same class when available; Main Cabin may be booked up to Y, while other cabins use the next available class in the same cabin.
  • Travel may be moved up to two days before or two days after the original date.
  • Use a Delta-marketed and Delta-operated or Delta Connection flight. Within 72 hours, limited same-day JV partner options may apply.
  • Reissue as an even exchange, add OSI DL REISSUE DUE TO DL INVOL, and notify the Pax. No waiver code is required.

Option 2: Pax Wants a Refund

Refund waiver code: OSI DL REFUND PER DL INVOL

Placement or process: Add the OSI message to the PNR, Before refund according to Delta's policy.

Important

  • Verify the ticket issue date because older tickets may have different thresholds.
  • The qualifying time conditions specifically include an earlier departure or later arrival.
  • Check whether Delta already revalidated the ticket before reissuing.
